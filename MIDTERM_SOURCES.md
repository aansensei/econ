# Midterm 1 sources and answer status

Primary source: the posted Module 1 resources of ECON 302, Canvas course 514581, accessed October 10, 2026.

The Midterm study page reuses the existing Chapters 1 to 4 and Chapter 5 potential banks. The downloadable source archive is under `midterm-assets`. The question bank is `midterm-data.js`.

The three posted old Midterm 1 sets are Fall 2016 Version 1, Spring 2017 Version 1 and Fall 2019 Version 2. A fresh source audit on October 11, 2026 checked all 113 multiple-choice questions and 14 short-answer questions. Fall 2016 has 36 MCQs and 5 short answers, Spring 2017 has 37 MCQs and 9 short answers, and Fall 2019 has 40 MCQs with no short-answer section. All question text, option order, shared environments and subparts were checked against the question documents. All 113 answer letters match the separate official keys. Full short-answer rubric text is available separately from the Vietnamese study explanations.

Question documents take precedence for the wording of answer choices. The Fall 2016 Q12 typo is preserved: A and B are identical in the original exam. The key corrects B and says all answers received credit. That correction is displayed in a separate note, and the question is not graded. The audit also restores the MPN term in Fall 2016 Q21 and the b/a ratio in Fall 2019 Q18 option D.

Whitespace and mathematical font variants are normalized for screen reading. Fractions, superscripts and subscripts use equivalent linear notation, with original PDF page links for comparison. The independent source snapshot and source-file hashes are in `tests/fixtures/old-midterm-source-audit.json`.

Answer labels distinguish official keys, locally checked problem-set answers, adapted More Practice MCQs, surveys, historical references and AI-generated practice. Ambiguous or outdated historical questions are not drawn into the current simulation. Full source documents remain accessible.

The posted instructor exclusions are Chapters 1 to 4 Pool 2 Question 16, Chapter 5 Pool 6 Question 6 and Chapter 5 Pool 7 Question 12. They are excluded from grading and simulation. The separate Chapters 1 to 4 question about constant wages and MRS has a condition warning and is also excluded from the Midterm simulation.

Completed problem-set captures include private attempt and grade information. Only sanitized question content and explanation guides are included here. Four missing Chapter 5 problem sets and the old online exam are marked TBA.

More Practice contains 14 automatically graded MCQs adapted from the original written and graph exercises. Each retains the original prompt for comparison and links to the source PDF and official answer video. Full video files and captions were not exported because no download control was found. The MCQ adaptations and Vietnamese explanations are model-derived, not represented as questions written by the instructor or official video transcripts.

Extended AI Questions contain 76 concept checks and 14 calculation items, separate from official questions. They are not included in the official-source simulation draw.

The simulation uses a study composition of 25 potential points, 19 related multiple-choice points and 6 self-assessed short-answer points, with a 60-minute deadline. It preserves paired math parts and their shared scenario. It does not claim to predict the exact exam format.
